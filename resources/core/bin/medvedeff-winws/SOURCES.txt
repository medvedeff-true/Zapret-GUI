medvedeff-winws uses the Windows engine from zapret, with modifications for
application-scoped traffic. The corresponding engine source is in
tools/medvedeff-winws in the Zapret-GUI source tree.

zapret (MIT): https://github.com/bol-van/zapret/tree/1e1d1e24c2ad5f9def2825e2eac7a89979a8a023
WinDivert 2.2 (see WINDIVERT-LICENSE.txt): https://github.com/basil00/WinDivert/tree/v2.2.2
Cygwin 3.6.12-1 (see CYGWIN-LICENSE.txt; LGPLv3 and GPLv3 texts
are included in WINDIVERT-LICENSE.txt): https://cygwin.com/packages/summary/cygwin-src.html
zlib 1.4-1 (see ZLIB-LICENSE.txt): https://cygwin.com/packages/summary/zlib-src.html

The accompanying DLLs are loaded dynamically; they are not merged into the executable.
